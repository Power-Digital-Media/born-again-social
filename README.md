# Born Again Social Studio

A dedicated Content Operating System & Campaign Generator for **Born Again Remodeling & Roofing**.

## Features
- **Field Job Ingestion**: Resilient sync with daily pin drops from `bornagainroofing.com`.
- **Privacy Firewall**: Strips exact GPS coordinates, residential street numbers, and customer details.
- **Smart Project Clustering**: Groups multi-pin check-ins from the same job into unified showcases.
- **Content Opportunity Scoring (COS)**: Ranks jobs (0–100) based on photo quantity, Before/After transformation, and recency.
- **Fact-Constrained AI Copy**: Generates platform-compliant packages for Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile (with zero phone numbers in GBP post body).
- **ChatGPT Creative Handoff**: 1-click `📦 Export for ChatGPT Creative` creates structured `chatgpt_creative_brief.json`, logo assets, and photo bundles for AI art direction.
- **Branded Canvas Engine**: Renders 4:5 Feed Frames and Before/After Split Frames.
